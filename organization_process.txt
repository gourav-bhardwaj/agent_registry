Process 1: Employee Onboarding
Purpose: Onboard a new employee and coordinate HR, IT, and the Assignee Team activities.
Steps:
1. Onboard the employee
    * Create the new employee record in the system.
    * Capture required details such as:
        * Employee name
        * Employee email
        * Phone number
        * Department
        * Designation
        * Joining date
        * Physical address
        * Assignee/team
2. Validate employee information
    * Validate whether all mandatory employee details are available.
    * If any required information is missing:
        * Send a notification to the employee’s email address.
        * Use followup@myorgemail.com as the sender.
        * Request the missing information.
    * Continue the onboarding process once the required information is available.
3. Notify HR
    * Send employee details to the HR department.
    * Recipient: hr@myorgemail.com
    * Include all relevant employee information required for HR processing.
4. Notify IT Support
    * Send employee details to IT Support.
    * Recipient: itsupport@myorgemail.com
    * Request assignment of:
        * Laptop
        * Monitor, if applicable
        * Keyboard/mouse
        * Other required accessories
    * Provide the employee’s physical address for delivery.
5. Coordinate Knowledge Transfer
    * Send a notification to the Assignee Team.
    * Ask the team to provide the date and time when they can start the employee’s KT (Knowledge Transfer).
6. Schedule KT Meeting
    * Once the Assignee Team provides the availability:
        * Schedule the KT meeting.
        * Add the newly onboarded employee and relevant team members.
        * Use Microsoft Teams as the meeting platform.
    * Send the meeting details to all participants.

Process 2: Employee Deboarding
Purpose: Safely remove an employee from the organization while coordinating HR, IT, and department responsibilities.
Steps:
1. Initiate employee deboarding
    * Identify the employee to be deboarded.
    * Retrieve the employee’s details from the system.
2. Human approval
    * Obtain human approval before proceeding with the deboarding operation.
3. Notify HR
    * Send employee deboarding details to HR.
    * Include:
        * Employee name
        * Employee email
        * Department
        * Last working date
        * Assignee/team
4. Notify IT Support
    * Notify IT Support about the employee’s deboarding.
    * Request:
        * Account/access deactivation
        * Laptop recovery
        * Accessory recovery
        * Organization asset collection
5. Notify the Assignee Team
    * Inform the employee’s current team about the deboarding.
    * Request any required KT or knowledge transfer to another team member.
6. Complete employee deboarding
    * After required approvals and coordination are completed:
        * Mark the employee as deboarded.
        * Update the employee’s status in the system.

Process 3: Department Onboarding
Purpose: Create and establish a new department within the organization.
Steps:
1. Collect department information
    * Obtain:
        * Department name
        * Department description
        * Department head/manager
        * Initial department strength
        * Required employees or roles
2. Create the department
    * Insert the new department into the organization system.
3. Validate department information
    * Verify that all mandatory department information has been provided.
    * If information is missing, request the required details from the requester.
4. Notify HR
    * Send the new department details to HR.
    * Include the department name, manager, purpose, and expected strength.
5. Notify relevant teams
    * Notify IT and other relevant organizational teams about the new department.
    * Request any infrastructure, access, or resource requirements.
6. Associate employees
    * Assign existing or newly onboarded employees to the new department as required.
7. Complete department setup
    * Verify that the department and its employees are correctly configured in the system.

Process 4: Department Dismantling
Purpose: Safely dismantle an existing department and handle its associated employees and resources.
Steps:
1. Identify the department
    * Retrieve the department details.
    * Retrieve all employees currently associated with the department.
2. Analyze employee impact
    * Determine where the employees will be moved.
    * Identify employees who are leaving the organization, if applicable.
3. Human approval
    * Obtain human approval before dismantling the department.
4. Notify HR
    * Send the department dismantling details to HR.
    * Include:
        * Department name
        * Department head
        * Current employee list
        * Proposed employee movements
5. Notify employees
    * Notify affected employees about the department restructuring.
    * Provide information about their new department or next assignment.
6. Move employees
    * Shift employees to their respective new departments.
7. Handle department resources
    * Notify IT and relevant teams about resources associated with the department.
    * Reassign or decommission department-specific resources as required.
8. Dismantle the department
    * Once all employees and dependencies have been handled:
        * Remove/deactivate the department from the system.

Process 5: Employee Department Transfer
Purpose: Shift an existing employee from one department to another while maintaining organizational consistency.
Steps:
1. Identify employee
    * Retrieve the employee’s details using their employee ID or email address.
2. Validate current department
    * Verify the employee’s current department.
3. Identify target department
    * Verify that the destination department exists.
    * Validate that the transfer is allowed.
4. Human approval
    * Obtain human approval before changing the employee’s department.
5. Update employee department
    * Update the employee record with the new department.
6. Notify HR
    * Send the transfer details to HR.
    * Include:
        * Employee name
        * Employee email
        * Previous department
        * New department
        * Effective date
7. Notify old department
    * Notify the employee’s previous department about the transfer.
8. Notify new department
    * Notify the destination department.
    * Provide the employee’s relevant details.
9. Coordinate KT
    * Ask the previous department/team whether KT is required.
    * If required, obtain the KT date and time.
10. Schedule KT
    * Schedule a Microsoft Teams meeting between the employee and relevant team members.
    * Send the meeting details to all participants.
11. Complete transfer
    * Verify that the employee is now associated with the target department.
    * Confirm that all relevant notifications and KT activities have been completed.

Process 6: Employee Detail Update
Purpose: Update existing employee information while ensuring the relevant stakeholders are informed.
Steps:
1. Identify the employee.
2. Retrieve the current employee details.
3. Identify the information that needs to be changed.
4. Obtain human approval before modifying employee details.
5. Update the employee record.
6. Notify HR about the updated employee information.
7. Notify other relevant teams if the change affects their responsibilities.
8. Verify that the updated information is correctly stored.

---

Process 7: Department Detail Update
Purpose: Update department information such as department name, department strength, or department manager.
Steps:
1. Identify the department.
2. Retrieve the existing department information.
3. Identify the fields that need to be changed.
4. Obtain human approval before modifying department information.
5. Update the department record.
6. Notify HR about the change.
7. Notify affected employees or teams if required.
8. Verify that the department information has been successfully updated.
